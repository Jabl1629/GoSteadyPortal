SMS Messaging Terms & Opt-In Consent

Last updated: June 1, 2026

GoSteady is a remote-monitoring service for elderly users of walking aids and their family caregivers. This page documents the GoSteady SMS (text message) program and how consumers provide consent to receive messages.

What messages we send

All GoSteady SMS are transactional and consumer-initiated. We do not send marketing or promotional content. There are two message types:

  • Account verification (2FA / one-time passcode): a one-time code so a user can verify their mobile number and securely sign in to the GoSteady app.
  • Account & activity notifications: occasional caregiver alerts about the person they monitor — for example, a low-activity check-in suggestion, or a device/account status alert.

How consumers opt in (consent)

A user or family caregiver signs up for GoSteady on our website. During sign-up they enter their own mobile number to verify it and to enable account & activity texts. The sign-up screen presents the consent disclosure below; by continuing, the consumer agrees to receive these messages. Consent is collected directly from the consumer who owns the number, and is never purchased, rented, or shared with third parties.

(555) 123-4567
By continuing you agree to receive account and alert texts from GoSteady. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Message frequency & rates

Verification codes are sent only when a user requests sign-in. Account & activity notifications are event-driven and occasional. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Sample messages

123456 is your GoSteady verification code. It expires in 5 minutes. Reply STOP to opt out.
GoSteady: walking activity for Mom was lower than usual today — you may want to check in. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opt-out & help

  • Opt out: reply STOP to any GoSteady text at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and then no further texts.
  • Help: reply HELP to any GoSteady text, or email jace@gosteady.co.

Privacy

Mobile numbers are used only to deliver the messages described above. We do not sell or share consumer mobile information or consent with third parties for their own marketing. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details, or contact jace@gosteady.co.

5. Assistance alerts & location

Some GoSteady devices include an assistance button. The assistance feature is optional and works only after it has been set up for a household, including consent to share location and at least one Care Circle member who has agreed to receive assistance alerts. When the button is pressed, the device counts down (currently 20 seconds), during which the request can be cancelled by holding the button down, and then sends an assistance request. GoSteady then attempts to reach each Care Circle member who has turned on assistance alerts by automated voice call and text message.

The device uses satellite positioning (GPS) only when the assistance button is pressed or a test is run — never to track the user at other times. Alerts include the most recent location available, with its approximate accuracy and when it was captured. A location may be unavailable, approximate, delayed, or out of date, and may arrive in a follow-up text.

The assistance feature depends on the device having power, cellular coverage, and a GPS signal; on our service providers and wireless carriers; and on Care Circle members' phones — any of which can fail or be delayed. GoSteady does not monitor assistance requests, does not contact 911, emergency services, or a monitoring center, and does not guarantee that any alert will be delivered, answered, or acted on.

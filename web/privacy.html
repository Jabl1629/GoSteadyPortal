Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GoSteady LLC ("GoSteady," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the GoSteady smart walker cap and the GoSteady app and website (together, the "Service"). GoSteady helps family caregivers and the people they care for understand daily walking activity.

SMS quick summary: we use your mobile number to send one-time login codes and account/activity notifications you sign up for. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or your SMS opt-in consent with any third party for their own marketing. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. See SMS Terms & Opt-In.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information you provide: name, email address, mobile phone number, and your relationship to the person using the walker.
  • Care-recipient information: a display name and details you provide about the person whose walking activity is monitored.
  • Activity & device data from the GoSteady cap: estimated steps, distance, and active time per walking session, derived movement metrics, and device-health data such as battery level, cellular signal, and firmware version. The cap measures motion with onboard sensors; it does not collect audio, images, or location/GPS.
  • Usage and technical data: log data, app/device identifiers, and diagnostics used to operate and improve the Service.

2. How we use information

  • Provide the Service — show walking-activity summaries and trends to authorized users.
  • Send account and activity communications you sign up for (including SMS — see §4).
  • Verify your identity and secure your account (one-time login codes / 2FA).
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the Service and our devices.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Health-related information

Walking-activity data can relate to a person's wellbeing. We treat it as sensitive and apply the protections in this policy. GoSteady is a general wellness and activity-monitoring product, not a medical or diagnostic service (see our Terms of Service). When GoSteady provides the Service to a healthcare facility under a separate agreement, additional data-protection terms (including HIPAA terms where applicable) govern that relationship.

4. SMS / text messaging

If you provide your mobile number and opt in, we send: (a) one-time login verification codes, and (b) account and activity notifications (for example, a low-activity check-in suggestion). Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or your SMS opt-in consent with third parties for their marketing purposes. SMS is delivered via our messaging provider solely to send the messages you signed up for.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With people you authorize — e.g., family members or caregivers you invite to view a care recipient's activity.
  • With service providers that operate the Service on our behalf (for example, cloud hosting and SMS delivery), under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing services to us.
  • For legal reasons — to comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the public.
  • In a business transfer — as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

6. Data security & retention

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information, including encryption in transit and at rest and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for legitimate business or legal purposes, then delete or de-identify it.

7. Your choices & rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your account information by contacting us.
  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (e.g., under U.S. state privacy laws) to access, delete, or limit use of your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising them. Contact us to make a request.

8. Children

The Service is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the updated version here and revise the effective date above; material changes will be communicated as required by law.
